Our Story

The PillBox Pharmacy Ltd. & Book Store was built with a simple mission: to provide our community with affordable, high-quality healthcare products and friendly, knowledgeable service.

What started as a small neighbourhood pharmacy has grown into a full-service wellness destination — stocking everything from prescription medicines and health supplements to books, stationery, perfumes, and personal care products.

We believe everyone deserves access to quality healthcare and everyday essentials in one convenient place, with staff who genuinely care about your wellbeing.
