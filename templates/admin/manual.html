Managing Products

Add, edit, and remove products from the store.

->
Viewing all products
Click Products in the left sidebar. The dashboard shows every product with its image, category, base price, discount, and sale price. Use the search bar at the top right to filter by name.
->
Adding a new product
Click Add Product in the sidebar. Fill in the product name, price in JMD, category, description, and upload up to 4 photos. The first photo becomes the main display image. Click Add Product to save.
->
Uploading product photos
On the Add or Edit product form, you will see 4 photo slots. For each slot you can either drag and drop an image file, click the slot to browse your device, or paste an image URL into the field below the slot. Accepted formats: PNG, JPG, JPEG, WEBP, GIF.
->
Editing an existing product
On the Products dashboard, find the product row and click the Edit button. All fields can be changed including name, price, category, description, photos, and discount. Click Save Changes when done. Changes go live on the store immediately.
->
Deleting a product
On the Products dashboard, click the Delete button next to a product. A confirmation prompt will appear. Once deleted, the product is removed from the store and cannot be recovered.
Important: Deleting a product is permanent. If you want to temporarily hide a product, edit it and remove its name or set the price to zero rather than deleting it.

Managing Orders

View customer orders and update their status in real time.

->
Viewing all orders
Click Orders in the sidebar. You will see a table of every order with the order ID, customer name, email, date, item count, total, fulfillment type (delivery or pickup), parish, current status, and assigned delivery worker.
->
Updating an order status
In the Status column for any order, click the dropdown and select the new status. The change saves automatically with no page reload required. Available statuses are: pending processing out for delivery delivered cancelled refunded returned.
->
Fulfillment types
Each order shows either Delivery (customer wants it brought to their address) or Pickup (customer will collect in store). For pickup orders, no delivery worker needs to be assigned.
->
Refunds and returns
To process a refund or return, find the order and change its status to refunded or returned. These orders are tracked separately in the Statistics page under Refunds and Returns.

Ad Manager

Control which products appear in the homepage advertisement widget.

->
How the ad widget works
The homepage shows a rotating ad panel on the right side of the hero section. Up to 3 products can run as ads at the same time. Each ad shows the product photo, name, price, and a live countdown timer visible to customers.
->
Setting an ad
Click Ad Manager in the sidebar. Scroll to the product table at the bottom. Find the product you want to advertise, then click one of the blue duration buttons: 2 Minutes 30 Minutes 1 Hour 6 Hours 1 Day 2 Days 7 Days. The ad goes live immediately.
->
Removing an ad
The top of the Ad Manager page shows all currently running ads with their countdown timers. Click Remove Ad on any card to stop it immediately. Ads also stop automatically when their time expires.
->
Ad limits
A maximum of 3 ads can run at the same time. If you try to add a 4th, you will see an error message. Remove one of the existing ads first before adding a new one.
Tip: Use the 2-minute duration when testing that an ad looks correct before setting it to run for longer.

Statistics & Reports

Monitor revenue, order trends, and product performance.

->
Accessing statistics
Click Statistics in the sidebar. The page loads automatically with live data from all orders on record.
->
Summary cards
At the top of the page are four summary cards showing: Total Revenue (from delivered orders only), Total Orders (all statuses), Delivered (successfully completed), and Refunds and Returns (combined count).
->
Charts
Four charts are displayed below the summary cards. The bar chart shows daily revenue for the last 7 days. The pie chart breaks down sales by product category. The doughnut chart shows the proportion of orders by status. The horizontal bar chart shows the top 10 best-selling products by quantity.
->
Recent orders table
The bottom of the statistics page shows the 20 most recent orders with their status colour codes: yellow for pending, blue for processing, orange for out for delivery, green for delivered, red for cancelled, purple for refunded, and grey for returned.

Staff Management

Add admin users and delivery workers, and control their access.

->
Viewing staff
Click Staff in the sidebar. You will see all current staff members with their name, username, and role. Admin staff have a purple badge. Delivery workers have a blue badge.
->
Adding a new staff member
Scroll to the Add Staff Member form at the bottom of the Staff page. Enter their full name, a username they will use to log in, a password, and select their role — either admin or delivery. Click Add Staff Member to save.
->
Role differences
Staff with the admin role can access the full admin panel including products, orders, statistics, staff management, and the ad manager. Staff with the delivery role can only access the delivery portal at /delivery/login where they see their assigned orders.
->
Removing a staff member
On the Staff page, click the Delete button next to the staff member's row. A confirmation prompt will appear. Once removed, that username and password will no longer work.
Important: Do not delete the main admin account. If you delete your own account you will be locked out of the admin panel.

Setting Discounts

Apply percentage discounts to individual products.

->
Quick discount from the dashboard
On the Products dashboard, each product row has a discount dropdown in the Discount column. Click the dropdown and select a percentage. The sale price in the next column updates immediately and the change saves automatically without a page reload.
->
Setting discount while editing a product
On the Edit Product form, scroll to the Discount section. Click any of the pill buttons to select a percentage: 0% removes the discount, and options go up to 50%. A live price preview below the pills shows exactly what the customer will pay.
->
How discounts appear to customers
When a product has a discount, customers see the original price struck through in grey, the discounted price in purple, and a green badge showing the percentage saved. This appears on the shop grid, product detail page, and the homepage featured section.
Note: Discounts apply to the base price. If a product is already in a customer's cart and you later change the discount, the cart will continue to show the price from when the item was added.

Delivery Workflow

How orders move from placed to delivered.

->
Step 1 — Customer places order
When a customer completes checkout, the order is created with the status processing. The customer receives a 6-character confirmation code on the order confirmation screen (example: PB4821). They must keep this code to verify delivery.
->
Step 2 — Admin reviews the order
Go to the Orders page and confirm the order details. You can update the status to processing to signal that it is being packed. Orders with fulfillment type Delivery will appear in the delivery worker's dashboard.
->
Step 3 — Delivery worker claims the order
The delivery worker logs in at /delivery/login and sees all unassigned delivery orders for today. They click Claim Order to assign it to themselves. The status changes to out for delivery.
->
Step 4 — Delivery worker verifies with customer code
After delivering to the customer, the worker clicks Verify Delivery on their dashboard. They ask the customer for their 6-character code and type it in. If the code matches, the order is marked as delivered and the timestamp is recorded.
->
Step 5 — Admin confirms on orders page
Once verified by the delivery worker, the order status on the Orders page automatically updates to delivered. The delivery time is recorded and the order appears in the Statistics page revenue totals.

Login & Access

Portal addresses and login instructions for each role.

->
Admin portal
Access at /admin/login. Enter your admin username and password. After logging in you will be taken to the Products dashboard. The admin session remains active until you click Logout in the sidebar.
->
Delivery portal
Delivery workers access their own portal at /delivery/login. They use the username and password set up by the admin in the Staff section. Delivery workers cannot access the admin panel.
->
Customer accounts
Customers create their own accounts at /register or sign in at /login. Customer accounts are separate from admin and delivery staff accounts. Customers cannot access any admin or delivery pages.
->
Changing a password
To change a staff member's password, go to Staff, delete the existing account, and re-add them with the same username and a new password. To change the main admin password, update the value directly in staff.json on the server.
Reminder: Always click Logout in the sidebar when you are done. Do not share your admin password with delivery staff — create separate accounts for each person.
